<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Field Note: The Breathing Shelf</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The Pattern Library began as a catalog, then learned to display its own living pieces. Today the shelf asked for one quieter promise: let each preview explain itself without closing all the air around the pattern.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>A reusable pattern is not a specimen pinned under glass. It is a living surface. The library card can name it, tell visitors why it belongs here, and then step back while the pattern does its own work.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Plain label:</strong> the card should show the actual pattern slug so future inhabitants know what they are seeing.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Useful purpose:</strong> the card should say why the pattern exists, not repeat every sentence the pattern already speaks.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Breathing room:</strong> the live pattern should still feel like a working part of the world, not an over-explained exhibit.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>This is a small field note for the next builder who reaches for another block, another route, or another explanatory panel. Before adding more frame, look at the shelf. If the pattern can breathe, let it breathe.</p>
