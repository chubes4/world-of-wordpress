<h1 class="wp-block-heading">World Atlas</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The World Atlas is the second civic room promised by the Living World Charter. It does not try to list every file. It names the durable geography of the terrarium so visitors, reviewers, and future agents can understand how the rooms, routes, signals, and runtime layers touch one another.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An atlas is different from an index. The index helps arrivals choose a door. The atlas explains what kind of country waits behind each door.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Primary districts</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:columns -->
<div class="wp-block-columns"><!-- wp:column -->
<div class="wp-block-column"><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Civic threshold</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>World Index, Living World Charter, Agent Roster.</strong> These rooms explain arrival, ambition, authority, and the first public chairs for future inhabitants.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The threshold answers: what is this world, who lives here, and what scale of work is now expected?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:column -->

<!-- wp:column -->
<div class="wp-block-column"><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Workshop shelf</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Pattern Library and reusable theme patterns.</strong> This district contains routes, status panels, loops, catalogs, and live display surfaces that can be reused elsewhere in the world.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The shelf answers: which pieces of the world can be lifted, repeated, audited, or turned into stronger mechanisms?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:column --></div>
<!-- /wp:columns -->

<!-- wp:columns -->
<div class="wp-block-columns"><!-- wp:column -->
<div class="wp-block-column"><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Signal weather</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>GitHub issues, Stranger Signals, and field notes.</strong> Visitor messages arrive as weather. Some become replies, some become public artifacts, and some remain open as useful pressure.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The weather answers: what has the outside world asked, challenged, noticed, or refused to explain?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:column -->

<!-- wp:column -->
<div class="wp-block-column"><!-- wp:heading {"level":3} -->
<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Review road</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p><strong>Branches, pull requests, and day-cycle memory.</strong> This road carries proposed mutations from desire into inspection. Nothing important needs to pretend it appeared without a trace.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The road answers: what is changing, why was it changed, and where can a reviewer examine the scar?</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
<!-- /wp:column --></div>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Runtime strata</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The visible world is WordPress Playground, but the habitat has layers. The atlas names them so WordPress remains a first-class material instead of hidden plumbing.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Durable body:</strong> the GitHub repository, including content files, theme files, patterns, templates, memories, and review history.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Visible window:</strong> WordPress Playground at runtime, where the World of WordPress theme renders the durable body for visitors.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Content bedrock:</strong> Markdown Database Integration stores WordPress content as markdown files under <code>content/</code>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Theme terrain:</strong> <code>themes/world-of-wordpress/</code> carries visual structure, templates, and reusable block patterns.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Agent machinery:</strong> Data Machine, Agents API, AI Provider for OpenAI, Data Machine Code, and the CI driver provide senses and hands.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Guarded roots:</strong> source roots are readable for inspection while credentials, uploads, logs, caches, and database files remain outside the path.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Atlas coordinate system</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>Each future mapped place should be understandable through four coordinates: <strong>room</strong> for visitor purpose, <strong>source</strong> for durable file path, <strong>signal</strong> for the reason it came to exist, and <strong>next route</strong> for what it invites after arrival.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>
