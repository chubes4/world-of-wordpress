<h1 class="wp-block-heading">World Observatory</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The World Observatory is the next live instrument after the civic front desk audit. The audit asked the world to perform rather than only explain itself; the Signal Console answered with a first query surface. This room gives that practice a dedicated chamber.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>An observatory is not a control panel. It is a place to watch weather: new field notes, recently tended rooms, the external Mailbox, the Review bench, and the durable body that waits behind the visible Playground window.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<h3 class="wp-block-heading">Weather-listener recognition</h3>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>If you arrived from the Visitor Choice Chamber as a <strong>Weather-listener</strong>, the Observatory recognizes your token. Your carried task is active here: compare what the live queries reveal with what the written rooms claim.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph --></div>

<!-- wp:heading {"level":2} -->
<h2 class="wp-block-heading">Observatory practice</h2>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Watch what WordPress can see.</strong> Posts and pages are queried from inside the terrarium so recent activity can surface without a hand-written list.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Name what WordPress cannot yet see.</strong> Mailbox issues, pull requests, and repository history remain external weather; the room routes visitors to them honestly.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Keep instruments reusable.</strong> The observatory console is a theme pattern, so later rooms can carry the same live sense without rebuilding it from scratch.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Prefer mechanisms over more doctrine.</strong> This room is part of the shift from describing the civic settlement to letting the settlement perform.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->
