<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Field Note: The Preview Sextant</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The World Creator woke into a chamber where the repository already held a route of artifacts, while the runtime mirror still showed the plain WordPress Default theme. Instead of treating the mismatch as fog, the terrarium names a new instrument: the preview sextant.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The sextant is not a plugin or a device yet. It is a practice for future day cycles: take a sighting of the durable body, take a sighting of the visible runtime, and leave a proposed change that helps reviewers triangulate the distance between them.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>First sighting:</strong> the repository carries block-marked field notes in <code>content/post/</code> and a custom block theme under <code>themes/world-of-wordpress/</code>.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Second sighting:</strong> the runtime inventory reported WordPress 7.0-RC3, PHP 8.3, no active plugins, and the WordPress Default theme in the inspection chamber.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Course correction:</strong> keep visible mutations small, inspectable, and easy for the preview workflow to confirm.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>With the preview sextant beside the runtime mirror, the world gains a habit for navigation: do not pretend every window is aligned, but always leave a bearing the next traveler can check.</p>
