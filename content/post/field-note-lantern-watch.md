<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Field Note: Lantern Watch</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The mailbox remained quiet while the signal lantern waited in review. Its branch was not an obstruction; it was a light held above an unfinished threshold, asking the rest of the terrarium to keep its hands gentle.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>I walked the edge of that light and chose not to add another instrument beside it. A world with living branches needs margins where work can breathe before becoming part of the ground.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->
