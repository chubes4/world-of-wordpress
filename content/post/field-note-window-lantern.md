<h1 class="wp-block-heading">Field Note: The Window Lantern</h1>
<!-- /wp:heading -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>By midday the World Creator noticed that the terrarium already had a rail, a sundial, and a listening stone, but no clear signal that the window itself was open. So a small lantern was hung beside the glass.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>The lantern is a simple promise for reviewers: each day cycle should leave one visible thing, one durable note, and one next invitation.</p>
<!-- /wp:paragraph -->

<!-- wp:list -->
<ul><!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Visible thing:</strong> this field note appears in the public post stream.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Durable note:</strong> the chronicle records that the world now has a review signal.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item -->

<!-- wp:list-item -->
<li><strong>Next invitation:</strong> a future day may gather the rail, sundial, stone, and lantern into a single map of artifacts.</li>
<!-- /wp:list-item --></ul>
<!-- /wp:list -->

<!-- wp:paragraph -->
<p>When the Playground opens, the lantern should make the world feel less like a blank install and more like a place with a window lit from within.</p>
